From Delhi to 3,000 metres, day by day.

The full trip from Delhi and back, each stop plotted by altitude. The Volvo leaves Delhi after 6 PM on day 1 and is back by about 11 AM on day 7.

Swipe to see all seven days →

  1. Day 1 216 m

    Delhi to Dharamshala

    Report at the boarding point in Delhi after 6 PM for a quick briefing, then the Volvo sets off for Dharamshala. Overnight journey into the hills.

  2. Day 2 2,082 m

    Dharamshala and McLeodganj

    Check in to the hotel and rest, then see the Dalai Lama Temple, Bhagsu Waterfall, St. John in the Wilderness Church and Mall Road, with time to shop for Tibetan handicrafts. Night at the hotel. Dinner included.

  3. Day 3 1,525 m

    Bir Billing

    Bir is known as the paragliding capital of India: you take off from Billing and land in Bir. Paragliding is optional, at extra cost. Then the local market, cafés and the Tibetan colony. Night at a camp or hotel in Bir. Breakfast and dinner included.

  4. Day 4 2,050 m

    Rafting in Kullu, then Manali

    On the drive to Manali, go river rafting in Kullu, included in the trip. In Manali, visit the Hidimba Devi Temple, Vashisht hot springs, Club House and Mall Road, then go café hopping in the evening. Night at the hotel. Breakfast and dinner included.

  5. Day 5 3,000 m

    Atal Tunnel and Solang Valley

    Head out to the Atal Tunnel and Solang Valley. Paragliding, zorbing, snow scooters and skiing are there if you want them, at extra cost. Back to Manali for the local market in the evening. Night at the hotel. Breakfast and dinner included.

  6. Day 6 1,580 m

    Kasol and Manikaran

    Drive from Manali to Kasol for its market and cafés, and visit the Manikaran Sahib Gurudwara, known for its hot springs. In the evening the Volvo leaves Kasol for Delhi. Breakfast included.

  7. Day 7 216 m

    Back in Delhi

    Reach Delhi by about 11 AM. Say goodbye to your new friends and start planning the reunion trip: there are so many places left to explore.

Questions people ask before booking.

Who is this trip for?

Glabol trips are only for 18–35 year olds, so you travel with a group of young people who are fun and easy to get along with. Getting friends to commit to a trip is hard, so plenty of people join solo when others back out. You may set off with a bunch of strangers, but you'll come back with a family.

Where does the trip start and end?

In Delhi. Report at the boarding point in Delhi after 6 PM on day 1, and the Volvo is back in Delhi by about 11 AM on day 7. Coming from another city? Book a flight or train that reaches Delhi by 2 PM on the start date, and one that leaves after 2 PM on the last day.

Where do we stay?

Four nights in the hills: one in Dharamshala, one at a camp or hotel in Bir and two in Manali. Nights 1 and 6 are on the overnight Volvo. Early check-in depends on availability.

How fit do I need to be?

There are no treks on this trip. You travel by Volvo and Tempo Traveller and walk around each stop. Paragliding, zorbing, skiing and the other adventure activities are optional.

Is paragliding included?

No. Paragliding in Bir Billing or Solang Valley is optional and at your own cost, like the other adventure activities in Solang. River rafting in Kullu and the bonfire night are included.

When do batches run?

Every week from late September 2026 to the end of May 2027, apart from the last two weeks of December, and all batches follow the same plan. If heavy snowfall or anything else closes a sight, it may be skipped: all sightseeing depends on availability.

What should I pack?

A down jacket, 2–3 woollen trek pants, 3 T-shirts, 2 woollen fleeces, waterproof woollen gloves, a sun cap, a woollen cap (plus a spare for layering), a woollen neck warmer or bandana, a quick-dry towel, undergarments and thermal innerwear. Carry an original ID (Aadhaar card, driving licence, voter ID or passport); there's no boarding without one. Pack light and leave valuables at home: Glabol isn't responsible if they're lost.

How do payment and cancellation work?

Pay the booking amount to confirm your seat and the rest at least 20 days before the trip, or the booking is cancelled automatically. Glabol also offers travel now, pay later; ask your trip captain how it works. The booking amount isn't refundable. Cancel 30–45 days before the start date and 50% of the trip cost is charged; within 30 days, 100%. You can move to another batch if you tell us at least 45 days before the trip, subject to availability.
