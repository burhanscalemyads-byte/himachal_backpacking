Full terms and cancellation policy

Terms and conditions

  • All bookings are on a first come, first served basis.
  • Bookings can't be transferred or adjusted. Only the names given at confirmation can travel.
  • You can reschedule if you tell us at least 45 days before the trip, subject to availability on your preferred dates.
  • If you cancel, the booking amount isn't refunded or adjusted in any case.
  • Photos and videos made on Glabol trips, by Glabol's content creators or by travellers, belong to Glabol and may be used in its advertising on any platform.
  • IDs are verified before boarding, and nobody boards without a valid government ID. Carry an original ID proof (Aadhaar card, driving licence, voter ID or passport) throughout the journey.
  • Glabol may rearrange the itinerary or transport because of unavoidable events such as weather, the political situation or local union disputes.
  • Glabol isn't responsible for man-made or natural calamities. All sports are safe, approved by the state ministry and run by trained professionals, but Glabol isn't liable for injuries or accidents during adventure sports or travel.
  • Tell Glabol in advance about any change to your booking.
  • If the weather is unsuitable or local government rules change, activities may be rescheduled or cancelled without notice.
  • Mischief or inappropriate behaviour isn't tolerated, and Glabol may cancel such bookings at any time during the trip without a refund.
  • Glabol can cancel the trip or an individual booking without prior notice.

Cancellation policy

  • Pay the remaining amount at least 20 days before the trip, or the system cancels the booking automatically.
  • The booking amount isn't refunded for any cancellation. On top of that, cancelling 30–45 days before the start date costs 50% of the trip cost, and cancelling within 30 days costs 100%.
  • If weather, government restrictions or the local administration or union cancel some activities, we'll try to arrange a feasible alternative, but there's no refund.
  • If government orders, harsh weather, protests, landslides or other unforeseen events disrupt the trip, we work out the best alternative plan, but no refunds are given.
  • Extra costs caused by natural calamities or unforeseen events, such as rain, snowfall, earthquakes, landslides, strikes or bandhs, are paid by the traveller.
  • Bookings and booking amounts can't be transferred or adjusted.
